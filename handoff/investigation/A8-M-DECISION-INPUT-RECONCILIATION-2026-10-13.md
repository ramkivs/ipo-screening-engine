# A8-M — Decision-Input Reconciliation (2026-10-13)

**Status:** Evidence-only reconciliation. A8-M and Phase 0 remain open; Phase 1 remains unauthorized. This note does not select a provider, derive a trend category or score, change a formula/configuration, or set missing-data behavior.

## What is approved—and what is not

The committed A8 decision record approves the **hybrid observation direction** for A8-M: use an official close for a metric established as session-defined; use a point-in-time (PIT) observation only under a separately approved rule for a metric that genuinely requires it; select the source, session/observation rule, and cutoff **per metric**. Its common safeguards require evidence of the actual event, preservation of original timestamps and timezone/offset alongside normalized UTC, separation of event/as-of, publication, retrieval, cutoff and evaluation times where applicable, and no invented freshness interval or cutoff. See `handoff/decisions/A8-final-snapshot-event-cutoffs-investigation.md` §§“Previously approved A8 principles” and “A8-M — Market” (lines 13–15, 29–35).

That approval does **not** decide whether NIFTY trend is session-defined or PIT, which index series or session applies, how `supportive`/`neutral`/`weak` are classified, a metric-specific cutoff or freshness interval, an approved source contract, or the outcome when data are absent or invalid. The pre-existing, untracked `handoff/investigation/A8-M-METRIC-DECISION-INPUT-2026-10-12.md` is an investigation—not an approval; its proposed transformation Options A/B/C and recommended Option C remain proposals (§§4.3–4.4, lines 107–149), and it is intentionally not included in this commit. Its statements describe that earlier inspection scope. The separate 2026-10-13 VNL package below adds later, still-unqualified listing observations without changing or replacing that historical record.

## Current checkout: configuration and runtime are legacy behavior, not A8 approval

This checkout is on the v1.5 configuration. It maps a supplied NIFTY category to points (`supportive` 2, `neutral` 1, `weak` 0); `market_regime_bucket` is configured as the pass-through `market.regime.nifty_trend`. Neither defines a close/SMA-based classification. `MarketRegimeAdapter` accepts a category string and normalizes legacy aliases (for example, `strong`/`bullish` to `supportive`); it does not calculate a category from index closes. An unrecognized label can pass through and is not thereby approved. References: `config/ipo-config.v1.5.0.json` lines 770–784, 1959–1964; `engine/ipo_screening/connectors/adapters.py` lines 283–317; `engine/ipo_screening/derived.py` lines 1774–1778.

| **Source and provenance** | Qualify the source and source-native record/event semantics per metric; retain the exact source record or bytes, record/session identity, original timestamp/timezone, publication/retrieval metadata, and required hashes. | **Keep NIFTY, VIX and listing gains separate** | No existing approved contract combines them. Keep the NIFTY bucket on its supplied NIFTY category; decide independently whether/how VIX is used; decide the listing-gain field’s own cohort and price contract. | Mixing them would be unsupported. The legacy generic market freshness path is elapsed-time based: v1.5 sets `market_staleness_hours` to 24 (`config/ipo-config.v1.5.0.json` lines 101–102); `FreshnessPolicy` uses a 24-hour market default and accepts up to one hour of future skew (`engine/ipo_screening/connectors/interfaces.py` lines 203–245). The market snapshot builder also uses an elapsed-hour limit and marks an as-of more than one hour ahead stale (`engine/ipo_screening/snapshots.py` lines 506–555). These are implementation/config defaults, not approved session-aware A8 limits.

For missing data, the general v1.5 convention is **UNKNOWN is not zero**: an UNKNOWN criterion contributes to the score range and reduces completeness; NOT_APPLICABLE is separate (`config/ipo-config.v1.5.0.json` lines 4–24). The scorer preserves UNKNOWN and rejects an unmapped categorical value rather than assigning points (`engine/ipo_screening/scoring.py` lines 217–261). Missing/non-fresh market blocks and absent fields also yield UNKNOWN (`engine/ipo_screening/derived.py` lines 1709–1722). The schema permits nullable trend and up to five nullable listing-gain entries, while the legacy listing variant needs five valid values. None of that chooses A8-M’s still-open **Final proceeds vs withheld** rule.
