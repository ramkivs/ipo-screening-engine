## About the Tonbo Imaging India IPO GMP

Its premium of +70.71% is the highest of the 19 Mainboard issues currently showing one. One lot costs ₹14,700, in line with the ₹14,949 median for Mainboard issues open alongside it. It has been subscribed 4.01×, more than 18 of the 72 Mainboard issues beside it.

The grey market premium shown above is what the unofficial market is currently paying over the issue price for a Tonbo Imaging India application. It is not an NSE or BSE price, it is not regulated, and it changes through the day. The estimated listing price is simply the issue price plus that premium — an indication, never a forecast of how the share will open.

Compare it with other [live Mainboard IPO GMP](https://www.papsdigital.com/ipo-gmp) before drawing a conclusion, and read the day-wise trend rather than a single snapshot — a premium drifting down into the close of an issue says more than its size on any one morning.

## Where these numbers come from

Every figure on this page is a published value from one named source, or N/A. Nothing is averaged, merged, inferred or filled in.

### The source

Grey market premium, price band, lot size, subscription and the IPO calendar are read from
[InvestorGain's public IPO GMP Live report](https://www.investorgain.com/report/ipo-gmp-live/331/),
and post-listing prices from its
[IPO GMP Performance report](https://www.investorgain.com/report/ipo-gmp-performance/377/).
There is no second source and no merged data, so a figure is either that source's published value or it is absent.
A field the source has not filled in shows N/A — never a substituted zero.

### Two different clocks

**"Source updated"** is the time InvestorGain itself stamped the row, in IST.
**"Updated N ago"** is how long since we last fetched it. They are not the same measurement and they
can disagree: a row fetched ten seconds ago can still carry a premium the source published an hour earlier.
When a refresh fails, the page says the figures are delayed rather than presenting the last set as current, and when
nothing can be verified it shows no figures at all rather than old ones.

### What is arithmetic, not data

Two columns are calculated here rather than fetched, using the source's own stated convention.
**Estimated listing price** is the upper end of the price band plus the current GMP.
