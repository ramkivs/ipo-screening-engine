### How to read today's IPO GMP

Read the premium against three things rather than on its own: the size of the issue price it sits on (₹30 on a ₹100 issue is a very different signal from ₹30 on a ₹1,000 issue, which is why GMP % is shown beside it), the direction it has moved over the last few sessions, and the subscription figure. A premium that is drifting down into the close of an issue is telling you something a single snapshot cannot. The day-wise trend for any IPO is on that IPO's own page.

### IPO GMP vs the actual listing price

These are different numbers and they frequently disagree. GMP is an expectation formed in a small market; the listing price is set by real demand on NSE or BSE on listing day. For every IPO on this page that has already listed, the actual listing price, the listing-day close and the current traded price are shown next to what the grey market had estimated — the honest way to see how well GMP has been tracking reality lately.

### Mainboard IPO GMP and SME IPO GMP

Mainboard IPOs are larger companies listing on the main NSE and BSE platforms. SME IPOs are smaller issues on the SME platforms, with much larger lot sizes and far thinner participation, so their premiums move further on less activity and deserve more caution. This page currently tracks 75 Mainboard IPOs and 93 SME IPOs, and you can filter to either at the top of the page.

### How accurate is IPO GMP?

Nobody publishing a GMP, including this page, can tell you how close it will land. A premium is what a small, unregulated market is paying today; it is not a forecast, and it can collapse between the close of an issue and its listing. Rather than take a claim about accuracy on trust, look at the recently listed issues on this page: each row shows the actual listing price and listing gain beside the issue price, and each IPO keeps a day-wise premium trend on its own page. ## IPO GMP FAQs

The questions people actually type, answered plainly — including the ones whose honest answer is "it does not".

Still unclear? Write to [contact@papsdigital.com](mailto:contact@papsdigital.com?subject=IPO%20GMP%20question) and a person will answer.

### What is IPO GMP?

IPO GMP (grey market premium) is the amount above the issue price at which an IPO application or allotment is changing hands in the unofficial grey market before the share lists on the exchange. A GMP of ₹30 on a ₹300 issue means the grey market is currently paying ₹330.

### What does grey market premium actually measure?

It measures what a small, private, unregulated market is willing to pay right now — nothing more. It is a sentiment reading taken from dealers, not a price discovered on NSE or BSE, and it carries no regulatory oversight or settlement guarantee.

### How is IPO GMP calculated into an estimated listing price?

Estimated listing price = IPO issue price (the upper end of the price band) + current GMP. Estimated profit or loss on one application = GMP × lot size. Both figures are arithmetic on an unofficial number, so they are indications, not predictions.

### Is IPO GMP official?

No. GMP is not published, verified or regulated by SEBI, NSE or BSE. It is collected from grey-market activity and can differ between sources and change through the day.

### Can IPO GMP predict the listing price?

No. GMP often moves in the same direction as listing performance, but it is regularly wrong in both size and direction — a high GMP has preceded a flat listing, and a modest one has preceded a strong open. Treat it as one input among many, never as a forecast.
