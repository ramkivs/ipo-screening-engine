## What is IPO GMP?

IPO GMP is the premium at which an IPO application or allotment changes hands in the grey market before the share lists. It exists because demand for a popular issue appears well before the exchange opens trading in it, and a small private market forms to price that demand. The premium moves with sentiment: subscription figures, anchor participation, the wider market's mood on the day, and how comparable recent IPOs actually listed.

### Grey market premium, in plain terms

The grey market is unofficial and unregulated. There is no exchange behind it, no settlement guarantee, and no regulator publishing the price.
