### What is the difference between Mainboard and SME IPO GMP?

Mainboard IPOs are larger companies listing on the main NSE/BSE platforms with wide participation. SME IPOs are smaller issues on the SME platforms, with larger lot sizes and much thinner trading. SME grey-market premiums tend to be more volatile and move on far less activity, so they should be read with more caution.

### Is it grey market premium or gray market premium?

Both spellings mean the same thing. "Grey market" is the British spelling used in Indian financial writing and by the exchanges, and "gray market" is the American one; GMP, grey market premium and gray market premium all refer to the same unofficial pre-listing price. This page uses the Indian spelling throughout.

### What is the Kostak rate in an IPO?

Kostak is the flat amount a grey-market buyer pays for your entire IPO application, agreed before allotment and paid whether or not you receive any shares. It is quoted per application, not per share: a kostak of ₹600 means you are paid ₹600 for the application itself, and the buyer keeps whatever the allotment turns out to be worth. It is an unofficial, unregulated private deal with no exchange, no settlement guarantee and no recourse.

### What is the difference between GMP and Kostak?

They price two different things. GMP is quoted per share and only means anything if shares are actually allotted — it is the premium over the issue price. Kostak is quoted per application and is paid regardless of allotment, so it is closer to selling the lottery ticket than the prize. A third arrangement, "Subject to Sauda", sits between them: a price agreed per application that is only paid if the application receives an allotment.

### What does "Subject to Sauda" mean?

It is a grey-market deal on an IPO application that only settles if the application is allotted. If you get no allotment, nothing is paid by either side; if you do, the agreed amount changes hands. That makes it riskier for the seller than a kostak, which pays either way, and cheaper for the buyer. Like kostak and GMP, it is unofficial and carries no regulatory protection.
