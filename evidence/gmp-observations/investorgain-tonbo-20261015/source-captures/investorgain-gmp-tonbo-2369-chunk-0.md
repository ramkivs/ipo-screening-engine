## IPO Grey Market Premium (GMP)

**The grey market premium (GMP)** is the price at which an IPO is traded in an unofficial/unregulated grey market prior to its listing. The GMP reflects how a particular company's IPO issue might react on the day of listing.A positive GMP signals that the IPO is likely to list at a profit, whilea negative GMP indicates a likely discount on listing.

IPO GMP is subject to extreme volatility — an investment decision based solely on **Tonbo Imaging IndiaIPO** GMP will prove risky. Consider all factors before deciding whether to invest inTonbo Imaging IndiaIPO.
