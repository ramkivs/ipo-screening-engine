## What this site is

MarketLiveIPOGMP tracks the grey market premium for Indian Mainboard and SME IPOs and presents it on a phone without a table you have to pinch-zoom. Every IPO gets price band, lot size, indicative listing price, subscription figures and the full dates timeline on one screen.

## Where the numbers come from

GMP quotes are collected from grey market dealer channels and cross-checked against other public trackers before publishing. Each reading is stored with a timestamp and never overwritten, which is why every IPO here has a GMP history chart rather than only a current number.

Subscription figures are read from the BSE and NSE public bidding pages during the bidding window. Allotment is checked on the registrar's own portal — we link there and never ask for your PAN or application number.

## How often it updates

Through the bidding window, GMP is refreshed several times a day and always carries a visible IST timestamp. If a number looks stale, the timestamp will say so — we would rather show an honest 4h ago than imply a freshness we do not have.

## What we do not do

We do not sell IPO applications, broker grey market trades, run a Kostak desk, or take money to move a number. We are not SEBI-registered investment advisers and nothing here is a recommendation to apply for or avoid any issue.

We publish listing outcomes for issues where GMP was wrong with the same prominence as the ones where it was right. A GMP tracker that only shows its wins is marketing, not data.
