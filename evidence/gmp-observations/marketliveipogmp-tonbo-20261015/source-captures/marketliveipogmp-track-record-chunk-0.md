## The short answer

Across 199 Indian IPOs that listed between January and October 2026 — 95 Mainboard and 104 SME — this site recorded a grey market premium before the issue listed and the price it actually opened at. GMP called the direction correctly on 150 of 177 issues that did not open exactly flat, or 85%. It was far less reliable on the size of the move: the median call was off by 7.0 percentage points and the average by 11.1, with only 83 of 199 landing within 5 points of the real listing. The premium was too optimistic on 105 issues and too pessimistic on 92, so the error is not a consistent bias you can correct for.

Put plainly: treat the premium as a read on sentiment, not as a price. It is reasonable evidence that an issue will open up rather than down. It is weak evidence of by how much, and on SME issues it can be wrong by more than the entire gain it predicted.
