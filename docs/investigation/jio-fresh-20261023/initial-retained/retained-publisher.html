Jio Platforms IPO is a book build issue of ₹30,213.00 crores. The issue is entirely a fresh issue of 27.00 crore shares of ₹30,213.00 crore.

Jio Platforms IPO bidding opened for subscription on Oct 21, 2026 and will close on Oct 23, 2026. The allotment for the Jio Platforms IPO is expected to be finalized on Oct 26, 2026. Jio Platforms IPO will list on NSE and BSE with a tentative listing date fixed as Oct 28, 2026.

Jio Platforms IPO is set issue price band at ₹1,065 to ₹1,119 per share. The lot size for an application is 12 shares. The minimum amount of investment required by an individual investor (retail) is ₹13,428 (12 shares) (based on upper price).

The issue includes a reservation of up to 46,94,836 shares for employees offered at a discount of ₹54.00 to the issue price.

Kotak Mahindra Capital Co.Ltd. is the book running lead manager and Kfin Technologies Ltd. is the registrar of the issue.

About Jio Platforms Ltd.

Updated on Oct 21, 2026

Incorporated on November 15, 2019, Jio Platforms Limited is a technology company focused on digital connectivity, digital services and technology-led solutions in India. The Company has developed proprietary digital technology and pan-India connectivity infrastructure to support the delivery of digital services to consumers and businesses. Its subsidiary, Reliance Jio Infocomm Limited, served 533.3 million customers as of June 30, 2026.The Company operates through an integrated technology platform encompassing digital connectivity, consumer applications, enterprise solutions, cloud services, Internet of Things (IoT) and artificial intelligence (AI). Its consumer offerings include mobile and fixed broadband, digital entertainment, cloud storage, virtual PC services and smart home solutions. For businesses, it provides enterprise connectivity, cloud and security solutions, managed services, IoT applications and AI-based solutions. The Company’s proprietary technology capabilities span network infrastructure, device engineering, software, operating systems and digital applications.

The Company’s infrastructure includes pan-India 4G and 5G connectivity, fibre connectivity across more than 1,200 facilities and over 100 edge nodes within its networks as of June 30, 2026. Its wireless broadband network covered more than 99% of India’s population. The Company and its subsidiaries had filed 7,220 patent applications, of which 1,043 had been granted, as of June 30, 2026. Its technology initiatives include an indigenous 5G technology stack, proprietary AI platform JioBrain and fixed wireless access technology. The Company also leverages a nationwide distribution network and partnerships to expand access to digital services.

As of June 30, 2026, the Company had 27,932 full-time employees and engaged 63,795 personnel on a contract basis. Its workforce includes teams across digital products and technology development, business operations, sales and distribution, customer service, network operations, and corporate functions.

Competitive Strengths

  • Leadership in digital connectivity and services across India.
  • Diversified portfolio of consumer and enterprise digital services.
  • Large and highly engaged customer base with nationwide reach.
  • Nationwide digital distribution network and strategic group synergies.
  • Proven ability to scale operations and deploy new technologies rapidly.
  • Extensive pan-India network infrastructure with 4G and 5G capabilities.
  • Proprietary technology platform with in-house engineering and full-stack capabilities.
  • Strong innovation capabilities supported by patents, AI and technology development.
